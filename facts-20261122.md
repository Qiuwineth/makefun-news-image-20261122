# Facts behind the poster — Sunday 22 November 2026

Every story on the poster was reported on, or reacted to on, 22 November
2026, and each was cross-checked against at least two independent
sources before it was drawn.

## Lead — Trilateral summit aftermath

- Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met for 90 minutes in
  Shenzhen on Thursday 19 November 2026 and issued a joint "declaration
  of peace" (nuclear war "cannot be won and must never be fought", more
  petroleum supply, UN reform, limits on ICC jurisdiction).
- On 21–22 November Trump hailed the meeting at the White House as "one
  of the most momentous summits in many years".
- Ukraine's President Zelensky called the summit "very unsuccessful for
  Russia", as Russia launched its biggest attacks of the year on
  Ukraine.
- The poster frames this as the aftermath and reactions — the meeting
  itself was on 19 November, not 22 November.
- Sources: Xinhua / People's Daily, 22 Nov 2026
  (en.people.cn/n3/2026/1122/c90000-20521815.html); BBC coverage,
  19–22 Nov 2026.

## Box 1 — FBI criminal database hacked (United States)

- The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its NCIC criminal
  database, which holds fingerprints and records of wanted and missing
  persons.
- President Trump confirmed the breach.
- Sources: BBC, 22 Nov 2026; CNBC, 22 Nov 2026.

## Box 2 — Indonesia factory fire

- A fire swept through a confectionery factory in Indonesia, killing
  3 people and leaving 36 missing, with dozens unaccounted for.
- Sources: BBC, 22 Nov 2026; Live World Daily news briefing,
  22 Nov 2026.

## Box 3 — Nigeria boat disaster

- A boat carrying mourners to a funeral capsized in southern Nigeria,
  killing at least 24 people.
- Sources: Al Jazeera, 22 Nov 2026; TFI news roundup, 22 Nov 2026.

## Box 4 — Verstappen wins the Las Vegas Grand Prix

- Max Verstappen (Red Bull) won an extraordinary Las Vegas Grand Prix
  from pole, by 0.639 seconds — his 75th Formula 1 win.
- The 50-lap race saw 34 lead changes, and Ferrari team-mates Lewis
  Hamilton and Charles Leclerc collided (lap 38), both collecting
  penalties.
- Sources: BBC, 22 Nov 2026; Sky Sports, 22 Nov 2026; Reuters,
  22 Nov 2026.

## Box 5 — South Korea education minister resigns

- Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned on Sunday 22 November, and
  President Lee Jae Myung accepted the resignation the same day.
- Choi took responsibility for the confusion caused by last-minute
  anti-cheating measures, rushed out over fears of AI-assisted cheating
  ahead of the CSAT (Suneung) college entrance exam; 111 suspected
  device-cheating cases were caught on exam day.
- Sources: BBC, 22 Nov 2026; Korea JoongAng Daily, 22 Nov 2026;
  Seoul Economic Daily, 22 Nov 2026.

## Box 6 — Nepal PM begins state visit to India

- Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah left Kathmandu on 22 November
  for a two-day state visit to India (22–23 November) at the invitation
  of Prime Minister Narendra Modi — his first bilateral visit abroad
  since taking office in March 2026.
- He is scheduled to call on President Droupadi Murmu and hold
  delegation-level talks with Modi on 23 November, covering trade,
  transit, energy, connectivity and infrastructure.
- Sources: Radio Nepal, 22 Nov 2026; IANS, 22 Nov 2026; Dainik Jagran
  English, 22 Nov 2026.
